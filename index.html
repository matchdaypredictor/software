FAQ

Questions people ask

Will it make me win every bet?

No, and nothing can. Sport is unpredictable and bookmakers build a margin into their odds. Matchday Predictor helps you find bets where the price is in your favour and avoid the ones where it isn't. Over many bets, that's what matters. Use the Backtest tab to judge each league yourself.

What's the difference between Matchday Predictor and PRO?

Matchday Predictor covers football in 12 top European leagues. PRO covers football in 33 leagues worldwide plus basketball (NBA, WNBA), tennis (ATP, WTA), ice hockey (NHL) and American football (NFL), and lets you set any goals, handicap or total line. Both have a 7-day free trial.

Do Matchday Predictor keys work on PRO?

No. Each app has its own licence keys. You can install both on the same device, and PRO can import your backup from Matchday Predictor so your saved results and bets come with you.

Does it need internet?

Only to download new results (about once a week) and to buy a licence. Predictions, backtests and your bet tracker all work offline.

Which leagues does it cover?

One-tap download for the English Premier League, Championship and League One, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga, Scottish Premiership, Belgian Pro League and Turkish Super Lig. You can add results for any other league, such as the NPFL, by hand or by CSV file. PRO adds 21 more football leagues, including Brazil, Argentina, the USA (MLS) and Japan.

How do I pay?

Tap Buy on WhatsApp inside the app or on this page. We'll send payment details, then your licence key. Activation takes a minute and works offline.

Can I use one licence on my phone and laptop?

Each licence works on one device only. To use Matchday Predictor on both your phone and laptop, buy a licence for each.

What if I change my phone?

Licences can't be moved between devices, so your new phone needs a new licence. Your saved matches and bets can come with you: use Export backup on the old phone and Import backup on the new one.

Is it a betting site?

No. Matchday Predictor is a statistics tool. It doesn't take bets, hold money or connect to any bookmaker. You place bets yourself with any licensed bookmaker.
